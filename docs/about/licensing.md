# Licensing

Vehicle Physics Pro is offered in three licensing tiers: **Community**, **Professional**, and **Enterprise**. All tiers include the full-featured Vehicle Controller and Wheel Collider components, plus essential add-ons.

- **[Community](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/physics/vehicle-physics-pro-community-edition-153556){: target="_blank" }** - Free, single-vehicle, desktop-only. Ideal for evaluation and small projects.
- **Professional** - Unlimited vehicles, all build targets, custom extensions. For indie and small-studio production.
- **Enterprise** - Full source code, specialized vehicle controllers, advanced editor tools, motion-platform support, all features. For studios and organizations.

### Subscription

The license to use VPP is perpetual after the first payment. An active subscription is required to receive
support and updates.

- One year of support and updates is included with the first payment.
- You may cancel at any time and keep the most recent VPP version available when the subscription expires.
- A 120-day grace period allows resubscribing at the yearly renewal rate after cancellation.
- Resubscribing more than 2 years after the subscription expired requires paying the full license fee again.

The subscription fee funds continuous development, feature updates, and long-term product support.

##### Germany-based users

- You can access the subscription cancellation form in the [PayPro Global Customer Portal](https://payproglobal.com/customer-support/){: target="_blank" }.
- To receive support and upgrades during the first year, you must actively confirm the yearly renewal plan
	when purchasing the license.
